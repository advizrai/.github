# Support

## Documentation

[docs.advizr.ca](https://docs.advizr.ca) covers the platform and the Academy.

## You are an Advizr client

Contact your delivery lead directly. That is the fastest route, and they can see your environment.

If you do not know who that is, email [info@advizr.ca](mailto:info@advizr.ca) with your company name.

## Something is broken

Open an issue on the repo it affects and use the bug form. Include what you expected, what happened,
and how to reproduce it.

If the repo is private and you cannot reach it, email [info@advizr.ca](mailto:info@advizr.ca).

## You found a security issue

Do not open an issue. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## You want to work with us

[Book a strategy call](https://calendly.com/advizrconsulting/ai-strategy-call) or email
[info@advizr.ca](mailto:info@advizr.ca).

## Everything else

[info@advizr.ca](mailto:info@advizr.ca). We are in Vancouver, BC, on Pacific time.
